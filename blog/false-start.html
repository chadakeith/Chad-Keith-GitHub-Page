False Start

Sometimes I have ideas, and they are great for about 30 minutes. If I can get away from the day-to day-busy life that most of us lead to foster the idea, I can make the idea into something nice. In the case of starting to post ideas, blogs, videos and such, I have false started again. I have so much floating around in my head, but most times it’s hard to define.

I feel like defining the problem is the absolute key. Anyone can come up with an idea, product, invention, or service and build that idea into a business. Yet having the key is the most important part. Is the idea “good,” meaning: is it unique enough to stand out? Is it a key that will open the door to success? That is where the question lies.

Working as a team has always been fun for me, so I hope to start adding some talented team members to help encourage me to put out more content that will bring up good conversations, networking, and business opportunities in the future.
